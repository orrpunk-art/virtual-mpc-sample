Computer speakers

Test tone → AUDIO IN

Patch AUDIO IN to Test tone, the computer's input or the loop cable from AUDIO OUT. REC GAIN on the back sets the level.

App output channels

The app drives 4 outputs. 1–2 follow MAIN VOLUME and play on the speaker and AUDIO OUT; 3–4 carry a second mix at a fixed level, played when PHONES goes to the computer (that it is the phones mix is an assumption). SYNC OUT is not emulated.

SD autorun packages

A zip with an autorun/ folder (a root-SSH package, a mod, your own script). The lab makes an exFAT card labelled MPCSAMPLE from it with the firmware's own fdisk/mkfs.exfat and inserts it, as a user would.
